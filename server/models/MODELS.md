## After Downloading

Place the `.glb` files in this `models/` folder.

The server (`server.py`) serves them at `/models` and the Flutter app loads them for AR overlay.

## Format

All models are in `.glb` (binary glTF) format, compatible with:
- Google ARCore (via Flutter)
- Three.js / Babylon.js
- Blender (import/export)
